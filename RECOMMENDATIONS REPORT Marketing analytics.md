**RECOMMENDATIONS REPORT**

**Marketing A/B Testing Analytics**

**Executive Summary**

An analysis of **582.15K** users **(558.88K treatment/ad group vs. 23.27K control/PSA group)** demonstrates that paid advertising delivers a statistically significant relative conversion lift of **\+48.29%** (p \< 0.05, Z \= 1.96 threshold cleared across peak hours). 

**Key Findings:**

* **Overall Incremental Lift:** The treatment(ad) group achieved a **2.42% CVR** versus **1.63% CVR** for the control(PSA) group, establishing an absolute lift of **\+0.79%** with no Sample Ratio Mismatch (SRM) detected.  
* **Frequency & Ad Saturation:** Low frequency exposure (\<15 ads) accounts for **65.14%** of users but yields an ad group CVR under **0.5%** (**1-5 ads**). Conversely, higher frequency tiers (**31-50+ ads**) drive exponential conversion gains (reaching **17.74% CVR** in the high bucket), proving high-intent engagement requires consistent repetition.  
* **Temporal & Day-Parting Dynamics:** Weekdays outperform weekends (**2.52% vs. 2.18% CVR**). Peak incremental performance occurs on Mondays (**3.15% ad CVR**, **\+56.02% lift**) and Tuesdays (**2.91% ad CVR**, **\+60.66% lift**). Hourly efficiency peaks during afternoon and evening windows (**2.00 PM – 9.00 PM**), peaking at **3.02% CVR** around **4:00 PM**.


**Media-Buying Recommendations**:

* **Implement Frequency Floor & Recapping Rules:** Reallocate budget away from single-exposure impressions. Establish a frequency floor of at least **6–15 impressions per user** and target ideal saturation at **16–50 ads** for high-value segments, as exposures under 5 ads generate negligible lift (**0.48% CVR**).  
* **Concentrate Budget on Early-Week Peak Windows:** Shift **65%–70% of weekly ad spend to Monday–Wednesday**, capitalizing on peak conversion rates (**3.15% on Monday** vs. **2.01% on Saturday**). Scale back weekend spending or restrict weekend delivery to remarketing pools.  
* **Prioritize High-Engagement Audience Retargeting:** The **High bucket** (50+ ads) represents only **3.01% of total users** (17.5K users) but delivers an impressive **17.42% CVR**. Build a dedicated retargeting audience segment for users who pass 30 impressions to ensure they receive consistent touchpoints until conversion, maximizing yield from your highest-intent prospect pool.    
* **Day-Parting Optimization (2:00 PM – 9:00 PM):** Execute automated bid multipliers (+20% to \+35%) during late afternoon and evening peak hours, where CVR consistently ranges between **2.68% and 3.02%**, while reducing bids by 40% during off-peak morning hours (1:00 AM – 6:00 AM).  
* **Capitalize on Mid-Week Incremental Lift Peaks:** While Monday yields the highest raw ad conversion rate (**3.15% CVR**), Tuesday and Wednesday deliver the highest **incremental lift** over the PSA baseline (**\+60.66% on Tuesday** and **\+56.02% on Monday/Wednesday**). Structure dynamic bid rules to prioritize impression share aggressively on Tuesday and Wednesday, where paid ads generate the maximum organic push over baseline behavior.
